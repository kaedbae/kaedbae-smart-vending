PAYMENT SUPPORT

Payment & Refund Questions

Tap a supported card or mobile wallet to unlock the cooler. After you take your items and close the door, your payment method is automatically charged for the products detected by the system.

When you tap, your bank may show a temporary authorization hold. This hold is released after the final charge posts and is not a second charge.

If you believe you were charged incorrectly, charged twice, or charged because of a machine error, contact KayBeeVending so we can review the transaction. We respond within 1 business day.

Please include the machine ID or location, the transaction date and approximate time, and the last four digits of the payment card if available. Do not email your full card number or security code.

Approved refunds are returned to your original payment method, usually within 5–10 business days depending on your bank.
