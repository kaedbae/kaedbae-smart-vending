COMMON QUESTIONS

Smart vending, explained.

How does the HAHA 440 know what someone takes?

Its vision system monitors changes to the shelves during a shopping session and uses those changes to determine the selected products.

Does the machine require our Wi-Fi?

The HAHA 440 is designed with cellular connectivity, reducing the need to connect to the host location's Wi-Fi network.

Can customers take several items at once?

Yes. The smart-cooler format is designed to recognize multiple items during the same shopping session.

Who handles stocking and service?

KaybeeVending coordinates machine placement, product stocking, monitoring, and ongoing service for approved locations.

Where does KaybeeVending operate?

We are accepting placement inquiries from businesses across Michigan. Availability depends on location and site requirements.
