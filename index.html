COMMON QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know.

How does getting a machine work?

Request a free evaluation and we'll contact you within 24 hours to review your location. Once approved, installation typically takes several days to a couple of weeks depending on machine availability and site requirements.

Is there any cost to my business?

No. For approved locations, KayBeeVending provides the machine, installation, stocking and routine maintenance at no cost to the host business. We earn revenue from purchases made at the machine.

Do we have to sign a long-term contract?

No minimum term is required under our standard placement agreement. We simply ask for 30 days' notice if the machine needs to be relocated or removed.

Who handles stocking and maintenance?

We do. KayBeeVending monitors inventory, restocks based on actual demand, maintains the machine and adjusts the product mix based on sales and feedback.

What does my location need to qualify?

We generally look for locations with approximately 50 or more people passing through daily. Offices, apartments, gyms, hotels, healthcare facilities, dealerships, manufacturing facilities and warehouses can all be good candidates. Our free site evaluation determines whether your location is a good fit.

Can we choose or change the products?

Yes. The product mix can be adjusted based on your preferences, customer feedback and actual sales. Most standard food and beverage items are typically priced between $1.50 and $4.00, with some premium products priced higher.

What happens after installation?

We continue monitoring, stocking and servicing the machine. About 30 days after installation, we'll also follow up with your site contact to review the product mix, restocking schedule and overall placement.
