Privacy Policy

KayBeeVending respects your privacy and uses the information you submit only to respond to your inquiry and evaluate vending placement opportunities.

Information we collect

When you submit our website form, we may collect your name, business or property name, email address, phone number, city, estimated daily traffic and any information you include in your message.

How we use your information

We use this information to contact you, evaluate whether your location may be suitable for a vending placement, discuss installation options and provide ongoing customer service.

Form processing

Our contact form is processed through Formspree, a third-party form service. Information submitted through the form may be processed by Formspree in order to deliver your inquiry to us.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers only when necessary to operate the website, process inquiries or provide requested services.

Data retention

We retain inquiry information only as long as reasonably necessary for business communication, service evaluation and recordkeeping.
