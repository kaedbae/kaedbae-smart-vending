Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Kaedbae LLC d/b/a KayBeeVending ("KayBeeVending," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information through kaybeevending.com.

Effective date: September 27, 2026

Information we collect

When you submit our website form, we may collect your name, business or property name, email address, phone number, Michigan city, approximate daily traffic, and any information you include in your message.

Our website hosting and service providers may also process basic technical information that is ordinarily transmitted when you visit a website, such as your IP address, browser or device information, referring page, and date and time of access.

How we use your information

We use information to respond to your inquiry, evaluate whether a location may be suitable for a vending placement, discuss installation and service options, provide customer service, maintain business records, protect the security and integrity of our website, and comply with legal obligations.

Contact form processing

Our contact form is processed through Formspree, a third-party service provider. Information submitted through the form may be processed by Formspree in order to transmit your inquiry to us.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers that help us operate our website, process inquiries, communicate with prospective or current host locations, maintain records, or provide related business services. We may also disclose information when required by law, legal process, or to protect our rights, property, users, or business.

Cookies and online tracking

At this time, KayBeeVending does not use advertising pixels or behavioral analytics tools such as Google Analytics or Meta Pixel on this website. Basic cookies or similar technologies may still be used by our hosting or service providers as necessary to operate and secure the website.

Because we do not currently use cross-site behavioral advertising or similar tracking technologies, we do not currently respond differently to browser "Do Not Track" signals.

Data retention

We retain inquiry information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to inquiries, evaluate and manage potential or existing business relationships, maintain appropriate business records, resolve disputes, and comply with legal obligations. We may delete information when it is no longer reasonably needed.

Your choices and requests

You may contact us to request that we review, correct, or delete personal information you previously submitted through this website. We will consider and respond to requests as required by applicable law and as reasonably practicable for our business records.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures intended to protect information submitted through the website. No method of transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

California visitors

Although KayBeeVending is based in Michigan and primarily serves Metro Detroit, California's Online Privacy Protection Act (CalOPPA) may apply when a commercial website collects personally identifiable information from California residents. This policy therefore describes the categories of information we collect, how we use and share it, how you may contact us regarding your information, and our current online-tracking practices.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will post the revised policy on this page and update the effective date above.
